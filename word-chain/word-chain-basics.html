Welcome to the ultimate word chain game basics guide! Whether you're new to word games or looking to improve, this guide covers everything you need to know.

How to Play Word Chain

The rules are simple: each new word must start with the last letter of the previous word. For example: apple → elephant → tiger → rabbit.

Tips for Beginners

  • Start with common letters like E, S, T
  • Keep track of used words
  • Use a dictionary for unknown words
