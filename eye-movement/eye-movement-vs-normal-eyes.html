# Eye Movement vs Normal Eyes - What's the Difference? Most people think their eyes move smoothly, but science tells a different story. ## The Science Behind Eye Movement ### Saccadic Eye Movement Your eyes naturally make small, quick jumps called **saccades** when tracking moving objects. This is normal human behavior. ### Smooth Pursuit **Smooth pursuit** is the ability to track moving objects without jerky movements. Only about 1% of people can do this naturally. ## Why the Eye Movement Challenge Went Viral The challenge appeals to everyone's curiosity: - Am I normal? - Can I control my eyes this way? - What does my score say about me? ## What High Scores Mean - **Psychopath Eyes**: People with high scores may have better sensorimotor integration - **Smooth Tracking**: Indicates good visual-motor coordination ## How to Improve Practice makes perfect!
