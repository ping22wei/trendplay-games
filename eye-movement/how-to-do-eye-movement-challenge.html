# How to Do the Eye Movement Challenge - Tips & Tricks The **Eye Movement Challenge** is one of the most viral TikTok games of September 2026. Here's how to master it and get that perfect smoothness score. ## What Is the Eye Movement Challenge? This challenge tests your ability to track moving objects with smooth, controlled eye movements. The goal is to follow a dot as it moves around the screen without jerky saccadic movements. ## Tips to Get a High Score 1. **Relax Your Eyes** - Tension causes jerky movements 2. **Blink Normally** - Don't force your eyes open 3. **Follow with Your Head Too** - Small head movements help 4. **Practice Makes Perfect** - Try multiple times ## Understanding the Score - **90%+**: Psychopath Eyes - unnaturally smooth - **70-89%**: Smooth Tracker - above average - **40-69%**: Average - normal human responses - **Below 40%**: Jerky Eyes - lots of saccades ## Related Games - [Upside Down Smile Challenge](/upside-down-smile/) - [Spoiled Brat Challenge](/spoiled-brat/)
