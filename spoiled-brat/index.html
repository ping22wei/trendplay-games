About the Spoiled Brat Challenge

The Spoiled Brat Challenge went viral on Snapchat and TikTok in September 2026. The game tests your ability to quickly identify objects that scream "entitled teenager" - think gold-plated toys, pink stress balls, and other ridiculously privileged items.

How to Play

Click the most spoiled-brat item from the options shown. You'll have only a few seconds per round! The faster and more accurately you pick, the higher your score. Can you spot what a truly spoiled brat would own?

What Makes an Item "Spoiled Brat"?

Look for items that are overly expensive, childish, or pretentious. Gold objects, pink everything, fancy toys, and anything that screams "I have too much money and too little personality" are usually correct answers!
