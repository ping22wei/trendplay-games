About the Upside Down Smile Challenge

The Upside Down Smile Challenge is a viral TikTok trend where participants try to smile while their head is completely upside down. When you flip your head upside, the natural muscle movements create a unique expression that looks like a mix between a smile and a frown - often described as "haunting" or "creepy-cute".

How to Play

Drag the sliders to match the target expression! Rotate your virtual face upside down and adjust the smile intensity. The game will tell you how close you got to the perfect "upside down smile" look. Try to get a match score above 90%!

Why Is It Hard?

When your head is upside down, gravity pulls your facial features in different directions. The orbicularis oris muscle (around your mouth) behaves differently when inverted, making it surprisingly difficult to create a natural-looking smile. This is why only about 1% of people can do it perfectly in real life!
