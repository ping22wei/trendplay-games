# How to Do the Upside Down Smile Challenge - Step by Step The Upside Down Smile Challenge is a viral TikTok trend where you try to smile with your head completely upside down. Here's how to master it. ## Why Is It So Hard? When you flip your head upside, gravity pulls your facial features in different directions. The orbicularis oris muscle behaves differently when inverted, creating a unique expression. ## Step-by-Step Guide ### Step 1: Get Ready Find a flat surface and position yourself so your head is completely upside down. ### Step 2: Relax Your Face Don't tense up - let your face hang naturally. ### Step 3: Try to Smile Attempt to create a smile while inverted. It will look different than a normal smile. ### Step 4: Take a Photo Have someone take a picture or use a timer. ## Tips for Success - Use a wall for support - Have someone help you position - Practice makes perfect - Don't rush - find your balance first ## What Makes It Viral The upside-down smile creates a "creepy-cute" expression that's both funny and slightly unsettling - perfect for social media. ## Related Games - [Eye Movement Challenge](/eye-movement/) - [Spoiled Brat Challenge](/spoiled-brat/)
